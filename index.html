Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Sleepy Tales ("we", "our", "the app") is a bedtime story generator that uses artificial intelligence. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

To create personalized bedtime stories, we collect the following information that you provide during setup:

  • Child's first name - used to personalize stories
  • Child's age - used to adjust story complexity
  • Child's gender - used to personalize story characters
  • Selected interests - used to choose story topics (e.g., dinosaurs, space, animals)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to generate personalized bedtime stories through our AI-powered service. Specifically:

  • Your child's name, age, gender, and interests are sent to our server to generate stories
  • Our server uses OpenAI (openai.com), a third-party AI service, to create story text, illustrations, and audio narration
  • Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS encryption

3. Third-Party AI Service

We use OpenAI's API to generate story content. When you generate a story:

  • Your child's first name, age, gender, and selected topic are sent to OpenAI
  • OpenAI processes this data solely to generate the requested story content
  • OpenAI does not use this data to train their models (per their API data usage policy)
  • Data is not stored by OpenAI beyond the time needed to process the request

For more information, see OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

  • On your device: Child profiles and generated stories are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • On our servers: We do not permanently store your personal data on our servers. Data is processed in real-time and not retained
  • By OpenAI: Data sent to OpenAI is not retained beyond the processing of each request

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties except:

  • OpenAI - as described above, solely for story generation
  • Apple - standard App Store analytics and subscription management

6. Children's Privacy

Sleepy Tales is designed to be used by parents or guardians to create stories for their children. We collect minimal information necessary to personalize stories. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental consent. The app is intended to be set up and operated by a parent or guardian.

7. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Delete your child's profile and all stored stories from the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Contact us to request information about your data

8. In-App Purchases

Sleepy Tales offers optional subscription plans managed through Apple's App Store. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
